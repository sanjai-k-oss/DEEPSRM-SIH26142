01 · WHY DEEPSRM

Open imagery is valuable. Fine spatial detail is the gap.

01

Medium resolution

Sentinel-2 provides open multispectral imagery, but many applications need finer spatial boundaries.

02

Interpolation is limited

Bilinear and bicubic upscaling can make images larger without recovering meaningful spatial structure.

03

Commercial imagery costs

Native high-resolution imagery can add procurement cost for large-scale monitoring.
